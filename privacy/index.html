Privacy Policy

Privacy information for Retro Goat Rampage on iPhone.

Summary

Retro Goat Rampage does not collect personal data, does not track users, does not show ads, and does not require an account.

Data Collection

Retro Goat Rampage is an offline arcade platform game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

  • No account registration.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no user profile data to request, export, or delete.

Changes

If the app changes in the future to include online services, analytics, ads, or account features, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
