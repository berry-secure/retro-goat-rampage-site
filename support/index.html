Retro Goat Rampage Privacy Policy

Support

Help page for Retro Goat Rampage on iPhone.

Quick Help

  • If touch controls do not respond correctly, fully close the app and open it again.
  • If a level feels stuck after pausing or backgrounding the app, restart the level from the pause menu.
  • If performance drops, close other heavy apps and make sure Low Power Mode is not limiting gameplay.
  • For the best experience, use an iPhone running iOS 18.5 or later.

Gameplay Tips

  • Build momentum before horn attacks to hit sheep harder.
  • Use tongue swings to cross wide gaps instead of relying only on jumps.
  • Power-ups are limited, so save stronger tools for dense enemy groups or boss phases.
  • Bosses change their attack patterns as the campaign moves from farms to the city tower.

Contact

If you need help with a crash, level issue, purchase concern, or App Store listing problem, open a support request at github.com/berry-secure/retro-goat-rampage-site/issues.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, build number, and a short description of what happened.

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal gameplay data. Do not send sensitive personal information in support requests.
